Formula

FV = S·(1+i)n + C·((1+i)n − 1) / i

i = (1 + 10.0%)1/12 − 1 · n = 180 months

  • Starts from ₹5 L already saved (S); contributions (C) added at each month end.
  • Assumes a constant 10.0% annual return, compounded monthly. Real returns vary year to year.
  • Nominal rupees: inflation is not applied, and taxes, fees and salary growth are not modelled.

Trust & privacy

Clear about what we do, and what we don’t know.

  • The demo never leaves your browser

    The live demo generates its sample data and runs every model in your browser, storing it in localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server in demo mode.

  • Real accounts use standard auth

    In full-stack mode, a FastAPI backend issues JWT sessions, and passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.

  • Projections are estimates

    Forecasts are model estimates built on stated assumptions (return, contributions, history). Each one shows its inputs, so you can judge it.

What finVerse doesn’t know

  • Accounts, loans or assets you haven’t added
  • Future changes to your income, job or family
  • Detailed tax treatment (only simplified where noted)
  • Market shocks, inflation spikes and rate changes

finVerse AI is an academic project for planning and education. It is not SEBI-registered investment advice; consider a registered adviser before acting on any projection.
